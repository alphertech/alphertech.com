Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining AlpherTech

What does the application fee cover?

The UGX 30,000 application fee covers administrative processing, skills verification, and onboarding support. It helps us ensure that serious freelancers join our network.

What types of freelance roles are available?

We welcome freelance developers (web, mobile, backend), UI/UX designers, content writers, digital marketers, data analysts, and project managers. If you have a skill you can offer remotely, we want to hear from you.

Do I need to be based in Uganda to apply?

No. We work with freelancers globally. While we are headquartered in Kampala, we welcome remote professionals from anywhere in the world.

How does AlpherTech find work for freelancers?

We connect our freelancers with clients through our network, partnerships, and inbound leads. We match your skills with relevant projects, so you can focus on doing great work.

What happens after I apply?

We review your application within 48 hours. If your skills match our needs, we'll invite you to complete the payment and onboard you to our freelancer platform. You'll then start receiving project opportunities.
