Installation Instructions

Follow these simple steps to install Plexus POS system on your Windows computer:

  1. Download the Windows installer from the option above.
  2. Run the installer and follow the setup wizard prompts.
  3. Launch Plexus and complete the initial security setup (PIN, Pattern, or Password).
  4. Configure your business details, currency (default: UGX), and tax settings.
  5. Start managing your business — all data is stored locally on your device.
