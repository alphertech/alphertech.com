Comprehensive Service Solutions

From web development to digital marketing, we offer complete services with transparent pricing and proven quality.

AlpherTech Solutions delivers end-to-end digital services for businesses, startups, and organizations across Kampala, Uganda, and beyond. From responsive websites and mobile apps to complete branding packages, our team combines technical expertise with practical design to help your business grow.

We specialize in web and app development, UI/UX design, graphics and branding, digital marketing, and hosting and cloud solutions. For remote clients, we offer pair programming, code reviews, international project management, and DevOps support across time zones.

Our additional services include hackathon organization, software and app testing, technical audits, and IT consulting. Backed by Seytrons — our free WiFi privacy app for Kampala — we understand local connectivity challenges and build solutions that perform reliably.

Security, transparency, and long-term partnership are at the core of our work. You get clear pricing, regular progress updates, and direct access to the project team from start to finish.
